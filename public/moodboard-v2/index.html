Four ways to make the landing page feel alive.

Each of the four prototypes below is a working scroll experience — not a concept deck. Open any card to feel it. They differ in what does the emotional work: the environment, the light in the room, the network of dots, or the surface of the water. Our job in this review is to pick one direction, or pair two, and hand Xclay a locked brief.

The four lenses · open any to scroll through
I
Lens I · Cinematography

The Room.

Same desk. Same window. Same chair. Only the light changes as you scroll — panic fluorescent, blue hour, dawn, noon, golden, candle.

  • Feels like Deakins, Malick, the late Kubrick.
  • Does the work through color temperature and shadow length.
  • Technique SVG scene · CSS color grade · canvas dust motes.
  • Partner reaction visceral — they feel the turn.
Open the Room 6 acts · 45s scroll
II
Lens II · Elemental

The Tide.

Water only. Storm sea → breaking waves → pond ripples → glass still → a single droplet. The same arc as the ecosystem, stripped to one element.

  • Feels like Sugimoto seascapes, private-bank calm.
  • Does the work through amplitude, rhythm, silence.
  • Technique canvas wave simulation · ripple physics.
  • Partner reaction meditative — they slow down.
Open the Tide 5 acts · 40s scroll
III
Lens III · Network

The Constellation.

Opens black. One dot. Scroll adds dots. Dots connect into lines. By the close you’re looking at a constellation — the one brain thesis, literally visualized as a star map being born.

  • Feels like Bloomberg data-sphere, star atlas.
  • Does the work by making the thesis visible.
  • Technique canvas nodes · distance-based edges · data pulses.
  • Partner reaction intellectual — they understand.
Open the Constellation 7 acts · 50s scroll
IV
Lens IV · Environmental

The Ecosystem.

Desert → sandstorm → lightning → dawn → garden → water → stillness. The fullest emotional arc. A whole world transforming beneath the reader as they scroll.

  • Feels like a nature documentary in stillframe.
  • Does the work through biome, sky, weather, color.
  • Technique canvas particles · 3-stop sky · lightning flashes.
  • Partner reaction emotional — they’re moved.
Open the Ecosystem 7 acts · 55s scroll
Director notes · how to read this mood board

Every lens is a different answer to the same question: what does “alive” mean on an Archio landing page?

The four prototypes are deliberately different kinds of alive. One pushes cinematography, one pushes elemental calm, one pushes intellectual clarity, one pushes emotion. Pick the lens that matches the single feeling a trader should have when they close their laptop after reading the page. We don’t need to build all four. We need to pick the one that makes the reader sit up straight.

  • If the goal is memorable & cinematic → Lens I · The Room.
  • If the goal is institutional & unhurried → Lens II · The Tide.
  • If the goal is technical & thesis-forward → Lens III · The Constellation.
  • If the goal is emotional & broad-reach → Lens IV · The Ecosystem.
