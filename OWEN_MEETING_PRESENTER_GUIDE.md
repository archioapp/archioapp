## EMERGENCY

"First, your read on whether we're attacking a real problem. If we are, we'd love to understand what you would prove next and how you would think about the integration side. Anything deeper than that we're open-minded about."

"Our current hypothesis is serious traders and mentor/operators first. The bigger question we're still testing is whether brokers or prop firms ultimately capture enough value from better engagement, retention and rule behavior to become the larger buyer."

"The accounts, community infrastructure, billing and AI engine are real. The room, forecast, rule check and review are built experiences using scripted or demo state. The record connecting the entire chain and broker ingestion are the next technical pieces."

**IF HE ASKS "WHY CAN'T TRADELOCKER BUILD THIS?"**
"You absolutely could. That's actually one of the reasons we wanted your perspective instead of pretending we know where that boundary belongs."

**IF HE OFFERS HELP / INTRO / NEXT STEP:**
"We'd love that. What would make the most sense as the next step?"

Do NOT negotiate equity, partnership economics or investment on this call.
