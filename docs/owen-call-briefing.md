## 0. CONTEXT — WHO, WHAT, WHY

WHO WE ARE
- Kan and [Partner]. Two traders who spent years learning the hard way — money lost, scams, rules broken because the rule lived in a different tab from the ticket — and started building the thing they wished existed.
- The product is ARCHIO: one workspace for the self-directed trader, built around the mentor's live call, the trader's decision, and a verified record.

WHO OWEN IS TO US
- Founder of TradeLocker, the execution platform half the prop-firm world trades on. Went from B2B website sales to one of the most successful businesses in the trading industry.
- Connected through Kan's contact Mahdi. We have wanted this meeting for a long time — it's a one-shot.

WHAT THE MEETING IS AND ISN'T
- It is NOT a fundraising pitch. We are not asking for money. We want his read, his ideas, his connections, any help — and if he offers involvement, we would explore it.
- It IS: make him feel the problem, make him see the loop, make him believe we two can execute. Everything that doesn't serve one of those three gets cut.
- We don't know how long he has. The structure must work at 10 minutes and at 45.

WHAT HE CARES ABOUT (our read)
- Founders who built it care about three things: (1) a problem they recognise from their own users, (2) a product that already looks worth building, (3) two people who know exactly what's real, what isn't, and what the next brick is. He will not be impressed by vision. He has seen a thousand decks.

## 1. THE ONE SENTENCE

"One workspace where the mentor's live call, the trader's decision, and the verified record share one memory."

The four verbs (the whole product): DISCOVER → LEARN LIVE → DECIDE → PROVE
- Discover: find the real mentor, the official room, the verified record — in one search.
- Learn live: the mentor's screen is the hero; every call lands on a timeline you can catch up on.
- Decide: chart centre, AI beside it, your rules in front of you before the ticket.
- Prove: a journal you didn't write yourself. A record that can't be edited after the fact.

Rule: say the sentence once, slowly, then stop talking. Three surfaces on the call, never nine systems. If he repeats it back in his own words, the frame worked — move on.

- Closing line: "The field isn't tilted by talent. It's tilted by who has a desk, a team and a record — and who has ten tabs."

THE TWO STORIES (to be written by the founders)

## 19. STRUCTURE OPTIONS WE CONSIDERED (and why we chose this one)

A. Deck-led pitch — familiar, safe; but he'd treat it as an investor pitch, sit back, and wait for the ask. One-directional.
B. Conversation with anchors — no slides for the first minutes, product shown live, a real question to him every ~8 minutes, slides only as backdrop.
C. Demo-first — strong opener, but without the "why" it reads as a feature pile, and "is this real data?" arrives before we're ahead of it.
D. Working session — "we want your brain on three decisions." Most respectful, most interactive; risk: he never sees the whole shape.

CHOSEN: B ending as D. Conversation with anchors, then hand him the wheel. Time-flex blocks so it works at 10, 25 or 45 minutes. Big idea vs. the mountain: neither — the loop plus proof we can build. UI quality is the execution proof; honesty about the gap is what makes a platform founder want to help.
