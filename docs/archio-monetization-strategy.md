# ARCHIO — Monetization Strategy (Decision Lab)

> **Purpose:** teach both founders the full money system *before* choosing a model, then lay
> out every credible option side-by-side with honest pros and cons. This is written to be
> **argued over**, not obeyed. Nothing here is a locked price. Real numbers come only after we
> talk to real mentors and traders.
>
> **The prime directive:** a useful product is not automatically a business. Monetization is a
> *design problem* — who gets value, who pays, at what moment paying feels fair, and what each
> choice quietly encourages or breaks.

---

## 1. The six actors (know who's in the room before pricing)

Money only makes sense once you separate the people. A "user" is not always a "payer," and a
"free user" is not automatically a cost with no value.

| Actor | Value they get | Money in/out | Cost they add for us | Why they stay | Abuse risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free trader** | Community, verified mentors, small import, one insight | Pays $0 | AI + storage cost | It's genuinely useful; friends are here | Farming free AI |
| **Paid trader** | Full memory, analysis, discipline tools | Pays monthly | Higher AI/support | ARCHIO makes them better | Cost creep |
| **Mentor / operator** | Runs a room, sees members, tools, verified record | Pays monthly (business expense) | Support, moderation load | Grows their business | Spam/low quality |
| **Creator / seller** | Sells communities, courses, agents, templates | Earns; we take a cut | Payments, trust, disputes | We bring the buyers | Selling junk |
| **Affiliate** | Earns for referrals that convert | Earns a share of revenue | Payout + fraud checks | Passive income | Fake referrals |
| **ARCHIO (us)** | The platform | Earns across all engines | Runs everything | It's the business | — |

**The key mental unlock:** free users are not freeloaders — they are the **inventory** that
makes mentors want to be here and makes communities feel alive. But they are only *worth* their
AI/storage cost if enough of them convert or attract payers. That tension is the whole game.

## 2. The four revenue engines (and when each can realistically start)

Almost every platform like ARCHIO uses **two or three of these at once**, layered over time.
No single engine wins.

| Engine | What it is | Resistance | Revenue speed | Best as | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Subscription** | Recurring monthly fee for premium | **High** (pay before fully sure) | **Immediate** | Day-one core revenue | A fast, obvious "magic moment" |
| **2. Usage / credits** | Pay for what you consume (esp. AI) | Medium (meters cause anxiety) | Immediate | Margin protection | Metering + telemetry |
| **3. Commerce take-rate** | Our cut when users sell to each other | **Lowest** (pay only after they earn) | **Slow** | Long-term biggest engine | A populated marketplace |
| **4. Partner / referral-in** | Brokers/tools pay us for qualified users | Low (no user cost) | Slow | Later bonus revenue | Scale + negotiating power |

**Plain-English on each:**
- **Subscription** = Netflix. Predictable, investors love it, but it's the highest wall to
  climb because you're asking for money before trust is fully earned.
- **Usage/credits** = a utility bill. Protects us from a power user running up a huge AI bill on
  a flat fee, but people *hate* meters and it slows usage.
- **Commerce** = the App Store / Etsy / Whop model. We don't sell; our *users* sell to each
  other and we keep a slice. Lowest resistance because **nobody pays until they've already made
  money.** But it earns ~nothing until the marketplace is busy.
- **Partner-in** = brokers/prop-firms/tools paying *us* to send them users. Lucrative later,
  requires scale we don't have yet.

## 3. The core principle: LOW resistance in, resistance rises at the moment of undeniable value

This is the heart of the founder's question ("high resistance or low resistance?"). The answer
is: **both, in sequence.**

> Resistance should be **low** to get *in* and *feel the magic*, and rise **only** at the exact
> moment the value becomes undeniable.

ARCHIO has a specific **magic moment**: the instant the AI reads a trader's real history and
tells them one true, uncomfortable thing about themselves.

- **Before** that moment, a paywall kills you — nobody pays for a promise.
- **After** that moment, a paywall *converts* — they've felt it and want more.

That's why **freemium** fits ARCHIO: free up to and including a taste of the magic; pay for
*depth* once they already believe. The two failure modes:
- **Everything free forever** → no revenue, AI costs sink you.
- **Everything paid** → empty rooms, no mentor comes, no magic moment ever happens.

Freemium threads that needle — which is exactly why Spotify, Discord, Notion, and Slack use it.

## 4. Free-boundary options — full side-by-side

How much works without paying? Six credible options. There is no single right answer; it's a
bet about what we value more (adoption speed vs revenue speed vs margin safety).

### Option 1 — Useful free core *(recommended starting hypothesis)*
- **Free experience:** join communities, follow verified mentors, small import, one real
  insight, limited Ask Archio.
- **Payment trigger:** unlimited memory / full analysis / operator tools.
- **Works because:** removes resistance, fills rooms, lets the magic moment happen.
- **Fails if:** free is *too* generous and nobody upgrades, or AI costs outrun conversion.
- **Resistance:** low · **Revenue speed:** medium · **Margin risk:** medium (cap AI) ·
  **Network effect:** strong · **Abuse:** medium.
- **Mitigation:** hard AI caps on free; the best "depth" features are paid.

### Option 2 — Narrow free (taste only)
- **Free:** community + one insight, then most intelligence is locked.
- **Works because:** pushes revenue sooner, protects margin.
- **Fails if:** users leave before feeling enough value; rooms stay thin.
- **Resistance:** medium-high · **Revenue:** faster · **Network effect:** weaker.

### Option 3 — Time-limited trial
- **Free:** everything for N days, then most of it locks.
- **Works because:** users feel the full magic; classic SaaS conversion.
- **Fails if:** traders need *longer* than a trial to accumulate enough trade history to see
  value — a real risk for ARCHIO specifically.
- **Resistance:** low-then-cliff · **Revenue:** fast · **Margin:** watch trial AI abuse.

### Option 4 — Community-sponsored access
- **Free for the student; the mentor pays** for members' premium features.
- **Works because:** removes *all* student resistance; mentor treats it as a business cost.
- **Fails if:** we lean on it too early before mentors trust the ROI.
- **Resistance (student):** near zero · **Revenue:** B2B, chunky · **Network effect:** strong.

### Option 5 — Creator-sponsored / seat-based
- Like #4 but priced per active student seat.
- **Works because:** scales with a mentor's success; very "fair-feeling."
- **Fails if:** seat definition is fuzzy or mentors game "active."
- **Complexity:** higher (metering seats).

### Option 6 — Mostly free, commerce-funded
- **Free:** almost everything; we earn on marketplace + partnerships.
- **Works because:** lowest possible resistance, huge top-of-funnel.
- **Fails if:** marketplace isn't busy yet → little/no revenue for a long time; AI costs bleed.
- **Resistance:** lowest · **Revenue:** slowest · **Margin:** dangerous early.

**Partner discussion prompts (don't force an answer — surface priorities):**
- Do we care more about *rooms full of people* or *revenue this quarter*?
- How long does a new trader need before ARCHIO can say something true about them? (This alone
  argues against a short trial.)
- How much monthly AI cost are we willing to eat to keep the free tier magical?

## 5. First-payer options — full arguments

Who do we design the first paid tier around? Each with why-it-works, why-it-fails, obstacles,
and mitigations.

### Path A — Mentor Pro first *(recommended anchor)*
- **Buyer pain:** running a trading community across scattered Discord/sheets is chaos; no
  proof of results.
- **What they buy:** organized rooms, member performance visibility, verified track record,
  mentor AI, growth tools.
- **Why it works:** one mentor brings 50–500 students (instant population), and they **expense**
  the cost like any business tool — far lower resistance than an individual paying personally.
- **Why it fails:** if we can't show the mentor real ROI (retention, growth, credibility) fast.
- **Obstacles:** mentors are busy, skeptical, loyal to existing tools.
- **Mitigations:** concierge onboarding for the first handful; make migration painless; lead
  with verification (the thing they *can't* get elsewhere).
- **Obligation created:** if mentors pay, their tools must actually work at launch.

### Path B — Trader Pro first
- **Buyer pain:** "why do I keep losing / repeating mistakes?"
- **What they buy:** personal AI memory, review, psychology/discipline tools.
- **Why it works:** huge audience, emotional pain, clear personal value.
- **Why it fails:** individuals resist personal subscriptions; needs the trade pipeline *and*
  the magic moment fully working first.
- **Mitigation:** Founding Trader pricing for early believers; nail the magic moment.

### Path C — Equal dual launch
- Both tiers, equal weight, day one.
- **Works:** more revenue paths. **Fails:** double the packaging/feature obligations at the
  moment we can least afford to split focus.

### Path D — Community-sponsored seats
- Mentors pay so their students get premium free.
- **Works:** powerful B2B motion, zero student resistance. **Fails:** niche to model first;
  needs mentors who already believe.

### Path E — Free first, paid later
- Launch entirely free, monetize once rooms are active.
- **Works:** max adoption. **Fails:** no revenue runway; AI costs; harder to introduce a wall
  later.

**Recommendation (a hypothesis, not a verdict):** **paired-loop launch** — **Mentor Pro as the
anchor offer**, with a **narrower Founding Trader Pro** alongside. Not equal scope, but both
able to pay. Rationale: mentors are the earliest reliable payer *and* they bring the users that
make everything else work.

## 6. Pricing-structure variations — genius options, side by side

We are **not** committing to prices. We're choosing a *structure* to test. Nine variations:

| # | Structure | Simplicity | Predictable | Margin safety | Friction | Scalability | Build complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free / Trader Pro / Mentor Pro + later marketplace fee *(recommended base)* | High | High | Med | Low | High | Low |
| 2 | Tiers + included AI allowance + overage caps | Med | High | **High** | Med | High | Med |
| 3 | Community-sponsored premium seats (mentor pays) | Med | Med | High | **Low (student)** | High | Med-High |
| 4 | Base subscription + paid add-on modules | Med | Med | Med | Med | High | Med |
| 5 | Commerce-first take-rate (mostly free) | High | Low | Low early | **Lowest** | Very High | Med |
| 6 | Creator SaaS + transaction fee hybrid | Med | Med | Med | Med | High | Med-High |
| 7 | Cohort / founding-member passes | High | Med | Med | Med | Med | Low |
| 8 | Enterprise / white-label community contracts (later) | Low | High | High | High | Med | High |
| 9 | **Recommended hybrid** (see below) | Med | High | High | Low | Very High | Med |

**#9 — the recommended hybrid, in words:**
- **Useful free network** layer (discovery, community, verified profiles, a taste of AI).
- **Mentor Pro** as the anchor paid offer.
- **Founding Trader Pro** — narrower, for early believers.
- **Included AI allowance** per tier (fair-use), *not* a visible per-message meter — protects
  margin without the anxiety of a meter.
- **Community-sponsored seats** as an *experiment* with willing mentors.
- **Commerce take-rate + affiliate payouts** turned on **later**, once buyers and sellers exist.

**Why the hybrid:** it gets low-resistance adoption (free), early reliable revenue (mentors),
margin protection (allowances), and a massive long-term engine (commerce) — timed so each
turns on only when its prerequisite exists.

**Could it be a combination / something cleverer?** Yes — that's the point of listing all nine.
The hybrid *is* a combination. The partner call should feel free to remix (e.g. "useful free +
sponsored seats, skip Trader Pro at launch").

## 7. Feature → payment logic (who pays for what, and why it's fair)

For each candidate feature: who pays, what job they're buying, and where it should live.

| Feature | Who pays | The job they buy | Free / Paid / Metered / Sponsored / Commerce | Why fair | Why it could backfire |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Import / sync (small) | Free | "See my trades in one place" | **Free** | Hook; low cost | — |
| Import / sync (unlimited) | Trader | "All my history, always synced" | **Paid** | Ongoing value | Feels like a tax if capped too low |
| First insight | Free | "Show me the magic" | **Free (1)** | Creates belief | Costs AI $ — cap it |
| Ask Archio (limited) | Free | "Try the co-pilot" | **Free (metered)** | Taste | Farming — hard cap |
| Ask Archio (full) | Trader | "My always-on analyst" | **Paid + allowance** | Core value | AI cost — allowance protects |
| Long-term memory | Trader | "It remembers *me*" | **Paid** | The moat | — |
| Trade Review / Morning Brief | Trader | "Coach me daily" | **Paid** | Habit value | — |
| Trading DNA / Mind Check | Trader | "Understand my psychology" | **Paid** | Differentiator | — |
| Verified track record | Mentor + Trader | "Prove my results" | **Paid** | Trust product | Verification cost/liability |
| Rooms / communities (join) | Free | "Be where my people are" | **Free** | Network fuel | Moderation load |
| Rooms (operate) | Mentor | "Run my business" | **Paid** | Business tool | Must actually work |
| Mentor analytics / Mentor AI | Mentor | "See & grow my members" | **Paid** | ROI | — |
| Storefronts / agents / templates | Creator | "Sell my stuff" | **Commerce (our cut)** | We bring buyers | Junk products |
| Execution bridge | (later) | "Trade from ARCHIO" | **Out of MVP** | Licensing | Huge risk — defer |
| Regional / enterprise controls | Enterprise | "Compliance & scale" | **Paid (later)** | High willingness | Complexity |

**Rule of thumb:** free = *belief-building + network fuel*; paid = *depth + operating a
business*; metered/allowance = *anything with real AI cost*; commerce = *anything a user sells
to another user*.

## 8. Affiliates & commerce — how "free app + payouts" actually works

The founder's exact confusion: *how do we pay affiliates if the app is free?*

**Answer:** you don't pay for a free signup. You pay when that person **spends money later.**

- I refer a friend → he joins free → ARCHIO owes me **$0** (correct — no money moved).
- Weeks later he buys Trader Pro or a mentor's community → **now** money moved → ARCHIO takes
  its cut and pays *me* a slice.

So "free" and "affiliate payouts" don't conflict: the free user is a **seed**; the affiliate is
paid from the **harvest**, not the planting. This also protects us — no cash goes out for
referrals that never became revenue.

**Affiliate model options:**
| Model | How | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time bounty | Fixed $ per first payment | Simple, capped | Weak long-term incentive |
| Recurring share | % of what the referral pays, ongoing | Strong incentive | Margin risk if forever |
| Fixed-window recurring | % for first 6–12 months | Balanced | Slightly complex |
| Marketplace seller referral | % of a creator's sales you drove | Aligns with GMV | Attribution work |
| Sponsored-seat economics | Mentor pays for student seats | B2B leverage | Metering |
| Two-sided reward | Both referrer and new user get value | Viral | Cost per activation |

**Guardrails (non-negotiable):** pay commissions from **net collected revenue** (after refunds,
payment fees, taxes, reserves); define an **attribution window**; block **self-referral/fraud**;
set a **payout threshold**; allow **clawback** on cancellations/chargebacks; **never** promise a
perpetual percentage before modeling the margin.

**Mutual benefit, one line each:**
- **Trader:** free entry, pays only for depth, can earn by referring.
- **Mentor:** brings students, runs a business, earns from sales, gets verified proof.
- **Creator:** reaches a real audience, keeps the majority of each sale.
- **Affiliate:** passive income from real conversions.
- **ARCHIO:** earns across subscriptions + commerce, aligned with everyone's success.

## 9. Unit economics — the formulas in plain English

Learn these once; they decide whether any model survives. (Examples use variables, not invented
customer behavior.)

- **Revenue per payer** = price × how long they stay. Longer retention beats a higher price.
- **Gross margin** = revenue − (AI + data + payments + support + storage). If free-tier AI cost
  per user > ~0, then *free users must convert or attract payers* or margin bleeds.
- **CAC** (cost to acquire a customer) vs **LTV** (lifetime value). **Rule of thumb: LTV should
  be ≥ 3× CAC**, and CAC should **pay back in months, not years.**
- **Marketplace:** revenue = **GMV × take-rate.** Small % of a big volume > big % of nothing —
  which is why commerce is a *later, scale* engine.
- **Contribution margin** = what one more user adds after *their* variable cost. If it's
  negative on free users with no conversion path, the free tier is a leak, not a funnel.

**Why AI allowances matter (the punchline):** every AI answer costs us real money. A flat
"unlimited AI" free tier can be bankrupted by a handful of power users. Allowances/caps keep the
free tier magical *and* survivable.

## 10. Decision gates & a 30/60/90 validation plan

We don't guess prices — we **test** them. No revenue *dates* are promised; instead, conditions:

- **First revenue is credible when:** the trade pipeline works + magic moment lands + a handful
  of mentors say "I'd pay for this" *and* put a card down (concierge pilot).
- **Repeatable revenue is credible when:** we see consistent free→paid conversion and
  acceptable retention across a cohort.
- **Marketplace revenue is credible when:** rooms are populated and creators have something to
  sell to a real audience.

**30/60/90 experiments:** mentor interviews → willingness-to-pay tests → concierge pilots →
pricing-page smoke tests → AI-cost telemetry → conversion/retention tracking. Each gate must
pass before we turn on the next engine.

## 11. The TradingView / market-data boundary (clean answer)

We are **not** charging for TradingView. We charge for **ARCHIO's brain**: trade memory,
psychology, verification, community tools, workflows. The chart is a **free embedded widget we
display**, properly attributed. We could remove the chart tomorrow and still have a paid
product, because **none of our paid features are the chart.**

- Embedded-widget terms, charting-library/commercial agreements, broker-integration
  requirements, and **independent market-data licensing** are *separate* issues.
- **Gate:** if we ever pipe in real-time data *outside* the widget, that's a data-rights /
  vendor / counsel question — confirm before pricing. We do **not** make a blanket compliance
  claim here.
